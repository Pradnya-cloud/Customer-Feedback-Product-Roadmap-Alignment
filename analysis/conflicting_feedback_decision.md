# Conflicting Feedback Decision Case

- Enterprise customer requests Feature A.
- 100 free users request Feature B.
- Sales requests Feature C.
- Engineering says Feature A requires major architecture changes.

## Product Manager Decision Framework
1. Identify the underlying problem behind each request.
2. Quantify affected customers and revenue exposure.
3. Compare retention risk and strategic alignment.
4. Validate whether the requesting segment matches the target market.
5. Assess qualified pipeline/revenue evidence for Sales requests.
6. Estimate engineering complexity and opportunity cost.
7. Run discovery where assumptions are uncertain.
8. Select the option with the strongest combined customer impact, business value, strategic fit and feasible effort.

Customer volume alone does not determine the roadmap.
