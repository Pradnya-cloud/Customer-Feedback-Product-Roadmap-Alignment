# Feedback Prioritization Methodology

## Decision Flow
CUSTOMER FEEDBACK → PAIN POINT → CUSTOMER IMPACT → BUSINESS VALUE → STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT → PRIORITY → ROADMAP DECISION

## Scoring
- Customer Impact: 1–5
- Business Value: revenue impact + retention risk + strategic alignment
- Strategic Alignment: 1–5
- Development Effort: 1–5
- Priority Score: weighted combination of impact, business value, alignment, frequency, severity and retention risk, adjusted for effort.

## Priority Classes
Critical | High Priority | Medium Priority | Low Priority | Monitor | Reject / Not Aligned

## Impact vs Effort
- High Impact + Low Effort = Quick Wins
- High Impact + High Effort = Strategic Initiatives
- Low Impact + Low Effort = Fill-ins
- Low Impact + High Effort = Avoid/Postpone
