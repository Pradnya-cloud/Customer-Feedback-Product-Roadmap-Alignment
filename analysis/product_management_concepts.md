# Product Management Concepts Used

| Concept | Application |
|---|---|
| Voice of Customer | Captures customer needs and pain points |
| Customer Discovery | Validates the problem behind requests |
| Customer Feedback Loop | Collect → analyze → decide → build → communicate → measure |
| Customer Pain Points | Converts friction into actionable problems |
| Product-Market Fit | Uses evidence of recurring customer value |
| Product Strategy | Filters opportunities through strategic themes |
| Product Vision | Long-term product direction |
| Product Roadmap | Converts priorities into Now / Next / Later |
| Backlog Management | Keeps ideas visible without overcommitting |
| Feature Prioritization | Ranks opportunities using evidence |
| Customer Retention | Highlights churn-risk signals |
| Customer Satisfaction | Uses CSAT |
| NPS | Measures promoters and detractors |
| Stakeholder Management | Balances customer, sales, success, engineering and business inputs |

## Feedback Source Quality
Interviews provide depth; surveys provide scale; NPS provides relationship-level signal; support tickets expose concrete problems; reviews reveal public sentiment; sales provides revenue context; Customer Success provides account and retention context; social/community provides emerging themes; usage data provides behavioral evidence.
