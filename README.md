# Customer Feedback & Product Roadmap Alignment Dashboard

## Project Overview
A practical Product Management portfolio project that converts Voice of Customer data into evidence-based feature prioritization and roadmap decisions.

## Business Problem
Customer feedback is distributed across interviews, surveys, NPS, support, reviews, sales, customer success, community and product usage. The product team needs a consistent system for turning this voice into decisions rather than building every request.

## Objective
Collect, clean, classify and analyze customer feedback; identify recurring pain points; measure sentiment, CSAT and NPS; quantify customer/business impact; prioritize product opportunities; align them with strategy; and create a Now / Next / Later roadmap.

## Product Scenario
SaaS product team involving Product Management, Product Owners, Product Analytics, Customer Success, Sales, Business Analysis and Engineering.

## Dataset
120 realistic synthetic customer feedback records.

## Core Decision Flow
CUSTOMER FEEDBACK → PAIN POINT → CUSTOMER IMPACT → BUSINESS VALUE → STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT → PRIORITY → ROADMAP DECISION

## Feedback Sources
Customer Interviews, Surveys, NPS Surveys, Support Tickets, Product Reviews, App Store Reviews, Sales Feedback, Customer Success, Social Media, Community Forums, Product Usage.

## Prioritization
Number of Customers Requesting, Segment Importance, Pain Severity, Customer Impact, Revenue Impact, Retention Risk, Strategic Alignment, Development Effort and Technical Complexity.

## Impact vs Effort
High Impact + Low Effort = Quick Wins  
High Impact + High Effort = Strategic Initiatives  
Low Impact + Low Effort = Fill-ins  
Low Impact + High Effort = Avoid/Postpone

## Dashboard
Executive KPI Cards; Sentiment; NPS/CSAT; Feature Ranking; Pain Points; Product Areas; Impact vs Effort; Feedback-to-Roadmap Funnel; Decision Table; Now/Next/Later; Filters.

## Key KPIs
Total Feedback: 120 | NPS: -49.2 | Avg CSAT: 3.32/5 | Positive Sentiment: 32.5% | Negative Sentiment: 43.3% | High-Severity Issues: 82 | High-Priority Requests: 45 | Churn-Risk Feedback: 34 | Roadmap Alignment: 68.3%

## AI-Assisted Analysis
AI may summarize feedback, detect sentiment, cluster similar requests, identify recurring themes and estimate urgency. AI supports Product Manager analysis; it does not make the final roadmap decision.
